How these results were produced

Every scan and fit on this page comes directly from Newtrinos.jl, run at a pinned commit, with no manual post-processing of the physics. The scripts below are the entire pipeline: one runs the likelihood scans and saves them, one converts that output for the plots above, and one runs a real profiled best-fit (nuisance parameters optimized, not fixed) to produce the pulls below.

compute_results.jl — runs the Newtrinos.jl scans

export_results.jl — converts the scan output to results.json

export_residuals.jl — exports per-bin pulls at each scan's own best-fit point

Newtrinos.jl results — generated from precomputed likelihood scans. See Newtrinos.jl for the analysis code.
