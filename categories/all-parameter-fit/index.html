All-parameter fit

A joint fit of all six oscillation parameters (θ₁₂, θ₁₃, θ₂₃, Δm²₂₁, Δm²₃₁, δCP) simultaneously, across every experiment — not yet available on this site.

Coming soon. The other pages on this site profile or scan one or two oscillation parameters at a time, with the rest fixed at nominal values — a true simultaneous 6-parameter grid scan would need roughly 21⁶ ≈ 85 million likelihood evaluations per experiment set, which isn't practical at the grid densities used elsewhere here. The planned approach is a Sobol-sequence (quasi-random) sampling of the full 6-dimensional parameter space, rather than a dense grid or a full BAT.jl nested-sampling posterior — giving good coverage of the joint parameter space at a fraction of the computational cost.
