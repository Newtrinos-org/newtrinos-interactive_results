Solar experiments

Constraints on neutrino oscillation from solar-neutrino experiments (e.g. Borexino, SNO) — not yet available on this site.

Coming soon. Newtrinos.jl does not yet have a solar-neutrino experiment module (no Borexino, SNO, or solar-flux physics) — this page will appear once one exists in Newtrinos.jl. In the meantime, the "solar" oscillation parameters (θ₁₂, Δm²₂₁) are still constrained on this site via the reactor experiments page, which includes KamLAND — a long-baseline reactor experiment sensitive to those same parameters.
